## Workplace Etiquette

<img width="1440" height="900" alt="Screenshot 2025-10-18 at 23 02 58" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6bdd5d79-1dc2-4fd5-afce-fa3d45dfea31" />

S – Situation

During my project group work, I was part of a diverse team where communication styles and work habits were different. One teammate often missed deadlines, which frustrated others and caused tension in our group meetings.

T – Task

I needed to maintain professionalism and ensure that the project was completed on time, while also helping improve the team’s communication and respect among members.

A – Action

I initiated a calm and private conversation with the teammate to understand their challenges rather than criticizing them publicly. I then suggested setting clear deadlines and using a shared task tracker so everyone could see progress. I also encouraged our team to use polite, constructive feedback during discussions and to listen before responding.

R – Result

The teammate appreciated the respectful approach and improved their time management. Our group became more cooperative and respectful of each other’s input, and we successfully submitted the project early. This experience strengthened my understanding of workplace etiquette—showing respect, clear communication, and professionalism even under pressure.
